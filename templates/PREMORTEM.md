# Premortem: {{plan or decision}}

Assume it is six months from now and this failed badly.

1. **Write the failure story** in 5 sentences, past tense. What exactly went wrong?
2. **List 8-10 independent causes.** Technical, legal, market, people, money, timing, dependencies.
3. **Rate each:** likelihood (1-5) x impact (1-5).
4. **For the top 3:** the earliest observable warning sign and one cheap action that reduces it now.
5. **Kill criterion:** the measurable result at which we stop instead of continuing.

Run it before launches, pricing changes, migrations and any decision that is expensive to undo.
